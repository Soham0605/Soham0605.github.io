About

I'm a PhD student in the Hennig Research Group at the University of Florida, working on first-principles and machine-learning methods for materials discovery. Most of my time goes into two things: extending how VASP models solvated interfaces, and screening zeolite structures for capturing xenon gas.

This site went up today, and I'll keep adding to it as the work moves along.

Research

VASPsol++

An extended implicit solvation code for VASP, built on non-linear, non-local electron-density overlap cavities. Developed for modeling electrochemical and semiconductor–liquid interfaces without system-specific empirical fitting.

Xenon capture in Ag-exchanged zeolites

High-throughput DFT and machine-learning interatomic potential screening of silver-exchanged zeolite frameworks, identifying stable adsorption environments and the atomistic mechanisms driving xenon selectivity.
